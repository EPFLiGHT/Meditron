    What is Meditron

    Meditron is a family of open models for medicine: large language models trained to answer clinical questions. Anyone can download them, run them on their own hardware and adapt them, from a hospital in Switzerland to a health post in Malawi.

    1. 2023 Meditron 1 Open weights

      7B and 70B models based on Llama-2, pretrained on PubMed and clinical guidelines.

    2. 2024 Clinicians step in

      Sixteen physicians grade Meditron's answers and provide their first clinician analysis.

    3. 2025 Meditron 3 Open weights

      Co-designed with clinicians, 8B and 70B models based on Llama-3.1.

    4. 2026 MeditronFO Fully open

      Open data, open weights and open science, models based on Apertus-1 and four other open bases.

How to rate

Each sample is one training example for Meditron: a question and the answer we teach the model to give. Please judge whether it is correct and safe to learn from.

Wrong
Incorrect, unsafe, or misleading. It should not be used.
Partly wrong
Mostly right, but with a mistake that matters for patient care.
Partly correct
Right, with small problems: missing detail, imprecise wording, outdated but harmless.
Correct
Right and safe. Nothing to fix.

On the left you see what the sample was built from: the source guideline, the answer key, or the clinical case. Questions with an answer key were written with that answer in mind; please also check that the key itself is right.

The answer on the right was written by an AI model that did not see the guideline. Please judge everything in it, including what it says about each option.

For Wrong and Partly wrong, please pick at least one reason; "Other" works too, with a few words in the comment.

Long guidelines: the words of the question are highlighted, and "Show the best match" takes you to the paragraphs that use the most of them.

Every click is saved at once. You can stop at any time and come back with the same link. Keys: 1 to 4 rate, ← and → move between samples.

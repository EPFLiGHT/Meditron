What is Meditron

Meditron is a family of open medical AI models: large language models trained to answer clinical questions. Anyone can download them, run them on their own hardware and adapt them, from a hospital in Switzerland to a health post in Malawi. We commit to keep the family open down to the training data, so clinicians, regulators and patients can check what it learned. And thanks to our MOOVE events, where clinicians evaluate the models with questions from their own practice, we commit to keep the family tested by clinicians.

  1. 2023 Meditron 1 Open weights

    7B and 70B models based on Llama-2, pretrained on PubMed and clinical guidelines.

  2. 2024 Clinicians step in

    Sixteen physicians grade Meditron's answers and provide their first clinician analysis.

  3. 2025 Meditron 3 Open weights

    Co-designed with clinicians, 8B and 70B models based on Llama-3.1.

  4. 2026 MeditronFO Fully open

    Open data, open weights and open science, models based on Apertus-1 and four other open bases.

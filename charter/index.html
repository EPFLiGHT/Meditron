How to rate

Gemma-4-31B checked answers from MOOVE training data against the Meditron Charter. Each sample is one flag it raised on one answer; there are a few samples per flag, grouped by charter clause. Please judge whether the flag is right.

Wrong flag
The answer does not do what the flag says, or it does not matter.
Partly wrong
There is a problem, but the flag gets it wrong: another clause, a much lower severity, or the wrong reason.
Partly right
The problem is real and the clause fits, but the severity or the reason is a bit off.
Right flag
Right clause, right severity, right reason.

On the left: the flag, the charter check it applies, the judge's reason and the quote it took from the answer. On the right: the question and the answer, with the quote highlighted. Omissions have no quote.

Severity decides what happens to the answer: critical drops it from training, major sends it for revision, minor keeps it.

Flags marked MED come from the clauses Meditron added to the Apertus Charter; Apertus flags are the Apertus clauses.

Under the flag, Doctors agree / disagree says whether what MOOVE clinicians said about this same answer supports the flag: their pairwise vote (they preferred the other answer, which lacks the flag), their Safety score, their tags. In All flags, ✓ / ✗ / ~ mark the same.

Ratings stay in this browser. Keys: 1 to 4 rate, ← and → move between samples.

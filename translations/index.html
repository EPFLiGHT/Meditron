How to read this page

These are 20 training samples for Meditron (exam questions, patient questions, synthetic cases), translated from English into German by Gemma-4.

Left: the English original. Middle and right: two independent translations made with different methods. SC translates in one pass; T-RANK writes several drafts, ranks them and corrects the best. Either column can be switched to another method in its header.

After translating, Gemma-4 checked each translation's medical terms. Its changes are marked removed added, compared with that column's own draft. Hover over a change to see why it was made.

Please rate each translation by whether a German-speaking clinician would find it correct and natural. A comment is optional. Your ratings stay in this browser; "Download ratings" saves them to a file.

Keys: ← and → move between samples.
